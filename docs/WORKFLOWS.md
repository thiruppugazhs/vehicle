# Vehicle Lifecycle Workflows

## 1. Asset Acquisition
- Registration and VIN cataloging
- Statutory document upload (RC, Insurance, PUC)
- Preventative schedule setup

## 2. Routine Maintenance Workflow
1. Scheduled service notification triggered.
2. Vehicle checked into authorized service center.
3. Labor, replaced components, and digital invoice recorded.
4. Reminder marked completed and next cycle projected.
